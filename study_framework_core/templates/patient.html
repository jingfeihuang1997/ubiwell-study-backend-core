Conversation history

Mind Guard (Alexa skill). Each question as Mind Guard asked it, and the words Alexa heard in reply. The skill sends the check-in after every answer, so a check-in that was cut off still shows what was said. It is matched to this participant by the Amazon account's email.

Parents: Mind Guard does not ask parents yet; their check-ins will show under Parents once it does.

Check-in results

Read from the Mind Guard check-ins in the range of the conversation history: under each measure is the question Mind Guard asks for it. Open a row to see, for every check-in, the exact questions and the words Alexa heard. The dot is red for severe, orange for moderate, green for mild or none, grey when it was not asked. Days counts from the first to the latest check-in in a row that reported it. Trend compares the two latest check-ins. These are the skill's own questions, not validated clinical scales, except the ASQ, the NIMH suicide-risk screen; a positive screen needs follow-up.
